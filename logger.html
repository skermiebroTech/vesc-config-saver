Refloat on-board recording

Refloat can record a few seconds at full loop speed inside the controller, with no Bluetooth dropouts. It records each time you ride and stops when you step off, so after a nosedive it holds the moment it happened. Needs firmware with data recording (Refloat Extras).

Saved logs

Read-only: the logger only asks the board for data. Downloading Refloat's recording pauses it, the same as Refloat's own screen does; it starts again next time you engage.
